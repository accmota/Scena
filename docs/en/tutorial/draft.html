Scena

Tutorials

Each tutorial builds a real script, step by step. For readers who already know basic Python. The English tutorials are being written; the rest are in Portuguese.

01

First script

Window, shapes, color, visual, sprite, keyboard, click and @when.
02

Catch stars

A bar, @every, a group, collision, destroy and a scoreboard.
03

Geometry

Handles, Anchor, live lines and a midpoint with a compass.
04

UI widgets

Slider, CheckBox, DropDown, Button, Menu and TextInput. Three scripts: the chain with a panel; a drawing with the mouse (Contrail, every); a banner that travels along a track (Track, move_along).
05

Ships

A simple space shooter: a ship, a fleet, shots from both sides — no classes.
Interlude

Groups

What a group can do: a set that keeps itself up to date, one order for all (destroy, move_by), questions (count, empty, nearest) and picking some (filter, count_where, any).
06

Ships II · Classes

Classes and objects: bubbles made from one mold, each with its own speed (setup, on_update, why not __init__); then the space shooter reorganized into classes, with a thruster (Jet).
07

Ships III · Atlas, explosion, lives

The pictures on one sheet (Sheet, frame), an explosion in frames (clip, play), sound and three lives.
08

Ships IV · Shield

A falling gift turns on a shield that wears out: active, @on_change, collision with info and an impact where the laser hit.
09

Formulas

A machine of linked shapes, like a spreadsheet: fields and formulas, brackets × dot (st["x"] × st.x), = × @=, @on_change, @when, unlink and the rules of the reactive system. Not a game.
10

Balls

Balls that bounce off the walls and off each other: resolve, vel, mass, a trail with Tail, camera zoom and a speed bar with @= and Colormap.
11

Interaction

The mouse in one place: click, hover (@hover), the right button, drag, drop on a trash can (@drop_by) and a grid that marks the cell under the mouse (Box, cell_at). At the end, the mouse on the canvas with no object at all (on_mouse_click, double click, wheel). No game: one feature per step.
Interlude

Hooks in the class

Each top-of-file hook inside a class: on_click, on_hover, @every and @leave_playfield for each object, @when(lambda self: …), and an Entity with no drawing that owns its own time.
12

Movement

A ship that goes where you click: place or travel (move_to with time= or speed=), speed up and slow down (ease=), facing forward (face=), the arrival event (on_done=), the handle of the trip (cancel) and a chase every frame (approach).
13

Tracks

A showcase of tracks (Track): ready-made shapes, your own paths, there and back (yoyo), a pendulum (ease), a queue (phase) and the tip that follows the path (face).
14

Orbits

A solar system: planets in orbit, a moon that leaves a trail of dots (History), a pulsing sun (tween) and a probe that points at the nearest planet (nearest, heading).
Interlude

Turn and grow

Turn to and turn by (rotate_to, rotate_by), the angle field and the point that stays still, about=, a box held by two anchors next to a loose one, and the same pair for size (scale_to, scale_by).
15

Plane

A plane between pillars: a sheet made of files with two clips, a control stick with inertia (chase), points, the crash and restarting.
16

Plane II · Depth

Scenery in layers that move at different speeds (parallax), filled forever by Endless; fire on the fall and music.
17

Plane III · Endless Landscape

A landscape that never repeats: random images, trees and houses built by a function (slot.rng) and a layer in front of the plane.
22

Studio · Piano

A piano on the keyboard and the mouse: a single note played at eight pitches (pitch=), keys that light up while the note sounds and an equalizer (audio.spectrum).
23

Studio II · Voice

Sound drives the screen: the microphone records your voice for the piano, a bar linked with @= shows how loud you speak, a star dances on a track (Track) and a clap sets off a sun.
24

Ships V · Chapters and sequences

The enemies get chapters (mode): formation, a dive with a jet, a fleet that arrives in sequence (async) and a boss that arrives, fights and falls in stages.
25

Ships VI · Acts

The game in acts (Act): title, level and game over, restarting with no cleanup code, the high score in st and the level as a sequence with until_empty.
