# Examples

Games, tools and visual pieces made with Scena. Every file is a complete
program: install Scena and run one.

```bash
pip install scena
python examples/ships_4_shield.py
```

That is from a clone of this repository. Without one, download
[`scena-examples.zip`](https://github.com/accmota/Scena/releases/latest/download/scena-examples.zip):
the examples of the latest release, with the images and sounds they use.

Each [tutorial](https://accmota.github.io/Scena/en/tutorial/) ends in one of
these files. The tutorial games come twice, the same program with its names in
English (`ships_4_shield.py`) and in Portuguese (`naves_4_escudo.py`), for the
[English](https://accmota.github.io/Scena/en/tutorial/) and the
[Portuguese](https://accmota.github.io/Scena/pt/tutorial/) tutorial. They are
written the way a learner writes: flat code, game data in `st`, no advanced
Python.

## Other examples

No tutorial ends in these; each docstring says what to try. Their names and
comments are in Portuguese, except `guardian.py`'s (in English).

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `entropia.py` | Two colours of balls mix once the middle wall goes; the panel counts each side every 5 s (mix, pressure, temperature, energy). Gravity, loose walls that the pressure blows off, a piston. |
| `app_pitagoras.py` | The Pythagorean theorem by rearranging triangles (drag, wheel to turn). |
| `constelacao.py` | Creative coding: drifting points thread to their nearest neighbour (`nearest`) into constellations that keep re-forming; Neon comets hunt the nearest free point and take its colour. Hold the mouse to pull the points, let go for a wave; SPACE adds a comet, T the screen trail. |
| `affinity.py` | Creative coding: invented atoms in a dish with two bonds. Strong bonds (valence, a fast enough collision) make molecules; weak bonds are strings between + and - ends of two molecules that snap and re-form with the heat. Charge comes from the bonds, so white on red bonds and white on amber does not, and the two separate. A soft shell and a steep core keep atoms from piling up; an ion lends charge to the atoms near it, so it holds neutral atoms too. Recipes Nascente / Coroas / Ilhas (1 2 3) and Acaso (R: random rules, ions included, side table); sliders for the atom count and each element's weight, and Rodar to build that mix; UP / DOWN heat, drag to stir, SPACE a spark. The atoms' numbers live on plain objects; the `Circle` only draws. |
| `revoada.py` | Flocking: 120 `Triangle` birds follow three rules (separate, align, gather) and flocks form with no leader. The heading is the colour, so a flock is one colour; the mouse is a falcon whose fright passes from bird to bird (white, thin); the sky lights up (`Bloom`) when the flock is in order. Click for a burst; S / A / J switch the rules. |
| `guardian.py` | An arcade defence game: a ship flies around a glowing ring and shoots outward; two pools of life (ship and ring). It opens on a typed story in plain text under a gradient title, with lights running around a frame (a track's `begin` / `end` window). Three geometric enemies (three translucent `Triangle`s whose wings breathe, `RegularPolygon`, `Gel`), each with its own shot and explosion (`burst_at`, `fireworks_at`, `debris_at`); a shot that meets an enemy shot zaps both. Mines cross the screen and some dive at the ring: a shot one's blast (`query_circle`) takes the enemies around it, other mines in a chain. The waves are a table of dicts read by one task (`run_task`), some with a faster `pace`: from one side the camera puts the ring at the far side of the screen (half off it on one wave) and turns twice in the game (clock marks show the turn); from many sides the enemies come in clusters, each out of its `Portal`; `WindStreaks` warn, `Embers` celebrate the swarm wave, and the victory is geometric: a four-point `Starburst`, ten small hexagons in the `aurora` preset (`Colormap.from_preset`) that open over the ring and then fly away as a tail (`@=` formulas on their center, radius and angle), and lights running around it on a circle `Track`; a defeat sets the ring on fire (a `Sunburst` swelling in it, `Sparks`, `Fire` tongues where the ship is and two `Fire` blazes leaning out). The playfield follows the camera (`canvas.playfield.set(canvas.game_view.outset(…))`), so a missed shot goes as soon as it leaves the screen. Pills give triple, rapid and heavy shots and a `Portal` shield that stops four hits; random pills are rationed and some waves leave longer-lasting pills for the next. In the swarm wave a mine comes early, timed to meet the swarm in front of the ring. A narrow radar (`query_sector`) pulls each shot onto an enemy almost in front. Sound effects in `name=` slots (one sound a volley, one boom a chain) and music. SPACE starts and shoots, ← / → fly. |
| `app_rgb_arena.py` | Particles of light (`GlowSpot`) whose colour is a three-pole charge: red pulls green, green pulls blue, blue pulls red, each pushes the one before. A panel creates, deletes and recolours particles and tunes the forces; drag to move one, the wheel zooms. |

## Showcases

Visual pieces that show what Scena can draw: art pieces and demoscene-style
shows, most of them looping forever. Unlike the rest of this folder, their
names and comments are in English. Each docstring says what to try.

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `genesis.py` | The life of a star in six chapters (`Act`s): nebula, accretion disc (light windows streaming down spiral arms), ignition, supernova (`explosion_at`, `ZoomBlur`, `Chroma`), pulsar (camera rotation locked to the beams), remnant. SPACE next chapter; click drops matter. |
| `tesla.py` | An electric garden at night in the rain: Tesla coils, cables where power runs as `begin`/`end` windows, lightning that strikes the tallest thing, a dawn. Drag the lightning rods to steer the strikes; click for a discharge; C for the control-room view (`Crt`). |
| `rose_window.py` | An endless dive into a stained-glass rose window: rings grown by φ in a `History`, counter-rotating glass, two lights walking a {7/3} star. Four movements (Glass, Mirror with `Kaleidoscope`, Dawn, Eclipse with a `difference` moon); 1–4, SPACE holds the dive, click strikes a pane. |
| `loom.py` | A Jacquard loom weaving a tapestry of light from punch cards written as strings at the top of the file. Click the card to punch or fill a hole; UP / DOWN speed; N next card. |
| `old_school.py` | A tribute megademo in eight parts, each classic effect rebuilt from Scena parts (starfield, sine scroller, copper bars, twister, rotozoomer, plasma and tunnel, vector balls, CRT power-off), run by one async storyboard, with music. |
| `pulse.py` | Music-driven visuals: shapes linked to `audio.spectrum()` bands, a beat detector on `@when`, three scenes. M switches to the microphone (VJ mode); 1–3 scenes. |
| `one_line.py` | One line of light that draws itself and turns into a star, a spiral, an eight and a wave; everything else on screen is read from it (`at(u)`, riders, a turning echo, rings). |
| `credits_roll.py` | End credits in which the engine thanks its parts: each feature performs under its own name (History, Concave, `@=`, the impacts, the camera, the looks…). |
| `demo100.py` | Rings of dancing triangles around a `Bolt` heart, in three parts (Open, Dance, Storm), written to be built up step by step. |
| `polygon_ballet.py` | A floor of regular polygons in three movements: domino waves that change their sides and looks, rosettes, and their edges as string art. |
| `string_theory.py` | Lines from simple rules: a times-table cardioid, moiré fans, Lissajous strings, harmonograph curves. Starts with a photosensitivity note (SPACE skips). |
| `xyz.py` | Circles, turtles and light, written to be built up step by step: a ring whose two lights run together, against and out of step (`begin`/`end`), a rosette of see-through squares, triangles and pentagons, turtle walks with sweep gradients and a line `Halo`, and rings inside the ring lit in step, out of phase and at different speeds. |
| `xyz2.py` | A ring of light and a vortex of glass, in under 100 lines of code: the ring's two lights (`begin`/`end`) run together, against and out of step; 24 see-through squares, each smaller and turned a little more, draw a spiral no square has, twist into a star, then take colours from a `Colormap` that slide along them. |
| `life.py` | Conway's Game of Life in glass: the grid's lines fly from a corner like a fan, newborn cells grow over a few ticks, the colour is the age (a `Colormap`), and a seed falls when the board stops changing. Click or drag flips cells; SPACE, RIGHT, R, C; `COLS` sets the board. |
| `canopy.py` | A tree of 511 branches laid by one recursive function that turns into a circuit, a coral and a fern; still dust and rising embers follow the drifting camera at different rates (`pan_response`), under `Bloom`, `Grade` and `ColorSweep`. |
| `pendulums.py` | A pendulum wave that sings: 24 bobs, each one more swing per cycle than the last, drift into snakes, rows and scatters and meet again; each plays a pentatonic note as it crosses the middle, and the row curls into a ring that draws flowers. |

## For contributors

A tutorial endpoint is the page's last step plus a docstring, nothing else
(`tests/test_tutorials.py` compares them): change the tutorial first, then copy
its last step here. The English file is the English page's last step;
`tools/tutorial/tutorial_parity.py` holds it to the Portuguese one, name for
name. Every program here runs headless in `tests/test_scripts_run.py`; a new
game joins this folder when it is ready.
